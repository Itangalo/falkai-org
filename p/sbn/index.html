Start exploring: five minutes a day

Work on your own for about fifteen minutes. Pick the entry that best matches what you actually do – not the one that sounds most advanced. You will not finish anything in fifteen minutes, and that is the point – this is about getting started. Afterwards, tell the person next to you what you found.

Tools mentioned in the talk

  • ClaudeStrong all-round chatbot. Paid subscription performs best.
  • ChatGPTStrong all-round chatbot. No login required. Paid subscription performs best.
  • GeminiAlmost as good, and well integrated with Google services.
  • Microsoft CopilotWidely available, but not as capable as the three above.
  • duck.aiFree, and the best of these for data privacy. No login required.
  • PerplexityWeb search with sources.
  • ElicitLiterature reviews.
  • ConsensusResearch-based answers to specific questions.
  • Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)Build a notebook from your own sources and ask questions about them. Generates overviews, infographics and presentation videos.
  • Arc SearchPhone browser with built-in AI summary functionality.
  • Obsidian Web ClipperSave web pages as Markdown with one click.
  • Claude Code and Claude CoworkAI agents that work on files on your own computer. Useful for far more than programming.
  • OpenAI Codex and ChatGPT WorkOpenAI's equivalents. Open-source alternatives also exist.
