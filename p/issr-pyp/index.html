Tools

  • ElicitFinds and compares research articles. Good when you want to know what the research actually says.
  • ConsensusResearch-based answers to a question, with the studies behind each claim.
  • PerplexitySearch with sources shown, for questions where you need to see where the answer came from.
  • Gemini NotebookUpload your own documents, then ask questions about them – or turn them into a podcast or an infographic.

After today
